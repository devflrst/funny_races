    Races from Around the World

    Some people race for cheese, some carry their wives, and some release thousands of rubber ducks into a river. Click on an image to go to Wikipedia for more details.

    🧀

    Cooper's Hill, Gloucestershire, England

    Cheese Rolling Race

    A wheel of cheese is launched down a steep hill, and participants chase after it. Catching it is almost impossible, so most people tumble head over heels. The first to reach the bottom wins the cheese.

    👫

    Sonkajärvi, Finland

    Wife Carrying Championship

    An obstacle course, your partner on your shoulders, and a prize of beer equal to her weight. The main thing is not to drop her.

    View on Wikipedia ↗

    England

    Lawn Mower Racing

    Blades are removed, engines are tuned, and a real championship is held. Speed is high, and the lawn's haircut is flawless.

    View on Wikipedia ↗

    🛁

    Dinant, Belgium

    Bathtub Regatta

    Crews decorate bathtubs like carnival floats and sail down the Meuse River. Sinking beautifully also counts.

    View on Wikipedia ↗

    🐌

    Congham, Norfolk, England

    World Snail Racing Championship

    Snails crawl from the center of a circle to its edge while spectators cheer them on. The one who doesn't change its mind wins.

    View on Wikipedia ↗

    🎃

    Windsor, Nova Scotia, Canada

    Pumpkin Regatta

    Giant pumpkins are hollowed out, put on the water, and raced by rowing. Growing such a pumpkin is already a feat.

    View on Wikipedia ↗

    Cardboard Boat Regattas

    A boat is glued together from cardboard and tape, then tested on real water. The winner is the one who floats, while the audience loves those who fall apart in the middle of the pond.

    View on Wikipedia ↗

    Rubber Duck Race

    Thousands of identical ducks are released downstream, and each one represents someone's ticket. Such races are usually held for charity.
